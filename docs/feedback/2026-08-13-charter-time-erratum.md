## Email body (plain text, his channel)

Subject: One correction to the charter, and why

Bruce,

Small correction to something in the charter, and it is worth two minutes because
it is the reason your morning number is right.

The charter says the dashboard updates at 9:45 in the morning. As of yesterday it
updates at 10:45 instead. I moved it, and here is why.

The government publishes the inflation and jobs numbers at 8:30 AM. At the time,
we read them only from the Federal Reserve's data service, which collects and
republishes them, and that service does not post the new figures until about
10:10.

So the old 9:45 run was 25 minutes too early, every single release morning. It
was not showing you a wrong number. It was showing you last month's number,
correctly labeled, on the one morning you would most want this month's.

Since then, per your "use the source" note, the dashboard also reads the
inflation and jobs figures straight from the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the
agency that publishes them. The 10:45 time stays: it is the anchor that
guarantees the morning run always lands after the new numbers exist, whichever
source answers first.

You saw this happen on Wednesday. The card showed June inflation at 2.6 percent
while July had already come out at 2.5 percent.

Moving to 10:45 fixes it. The cost is that the morning briefing lands an hour
later on ordinary days. I think that is a good trade for having the right number
on the two mornings a month that actually matter, but tell me if you would rather
have it early.

Nothing else in the charter changes.

Arash
